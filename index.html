What it is

What Babel does

Babel is a catalog-driven CLI that validates and previews prompt and instruction stacks before a model ever runs them. It reports typecheck, catalog, benchmark, and workspace-health results as typed diagnostics a reviewer can read.

Scope: a validation, preview, and diagnostics tool, not operational agent infrastructure.

Not claimed: broad autonomous coding-agent reliability or a fixed catalog count.
